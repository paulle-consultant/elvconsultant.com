Kỳ 2027 hứa hẹn còn lớn hơn.

Secutech Vietnam 2026 reflects Vietnam market maturity: first-ever integration of Smart Building with Security exhibition, confirming the convergence of ELV+ICT+BMS in new construction projects. With 480 brands and 6 cross-sector themes, this is a strong market signal that Vietnamese customers seek comprehensive integrated solutions over siloed systems. The 2027 edition promises to be even larger.
